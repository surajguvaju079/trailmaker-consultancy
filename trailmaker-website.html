What we do

Every stage of the journey, covered.

From the first "where should I even apply" conversation to the day you land, our advisors carry the file — so nothing slips between counseling, testing, and paperwork.

Study abroad counseling

One-on-one profile assessment to match your goals, budget, and grades to the right country and course.

IELTS / PTE / SAT prep

Small-batch and one-to-one classes with mock tests, taught by trainers who track your score weekly.

Application & scholarships

SOPs, LORs, and forms handled end-to-end, plus a search for scholarships you actually qualify for.

Visa & pre-departure

Mock interviews, document checks, and a briefing on housing, insurance, and life in your new city.
